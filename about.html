Core VLSI Interests

My technical focus centers on the full spectrum of the front-end and physical design flow:

  • •
    RTL Design: Translating complex digital architectures and FSMs into synthesizable Verilog modules.
  • •
    Digital Verification: Creating rigorous testbenches, self-checking assertions, and analyzing waveform coverage.
  • •
    CMOS Circuit Design: Examining VTC curves, transistor sizing, noise margins, and low-power characteristics.
  • •
    FPGA Prototyping: Board-level synthesis, pin mapping, clock constraints, and hardware emulation on FPGA kits.
  • •
    Synthesis & Static Timing Analysis: Understanding setup/hold margins, clock skew, critical paths, and gate delays.
